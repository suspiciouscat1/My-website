About Us

Sugar Craving Bakehouse started off as a small online home business in Bahrain, created with a passion for baking fresh, homemade treats. What began as simple batches made from a home kitchen slowly grew through word of mouth, love from customers,and a dedication to quality. We officially opened a physical store on January 8th, 2026, where every item is made with care.

See More

We pride ourselves in being able to satisfy any sweet craving you may have, whether it’s something rich, soft, crunchy, or completely indulgent. From classic favourites to unique flavour combinations, every treat is baked fresh to bring comfort and joy in every bite. Alongside our desserts, we aim to provide an excellent and welcoming experience for every customer who walks through our doors or orders online.
